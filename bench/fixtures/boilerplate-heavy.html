Local Farmers Market Returns This Weekend

The Millbrook Farmers Market opens for its 2026 season this Saturday at Town Square, running every weekend through October with produce, baked goods, and crafts from over thirty local vendors.

New this year is a dedicated stall row for first-time vendors, part of an effort by the town council to make the market more accessible to smaller growers.
