A Complete Guide to Itemizing Tax Deductions

By Wallace Chen, CPA · Money & Filing

Most filers take the standard deduction because it's simpler, but if your deductible expenses run high enough, itemizing can meaningfully lower your taxable income. The most commonly overlooked category is unreimbursed medical expenses above 7.5% of adjusted gross income.

Mortgage interest and state and local taxes remain the two largest itemized categories for most households, though the cap on state and local tax deductions limits how much high-tax-state residents can claim.

Charitable contributions are worth tracking carefully throughout the year rather than reconstructing them in April — keep receipts for cash donations over $250 and get a written acknowledgment from the organization.

Run the numbers both ways before filing. A quick comparison against the standard deduction takes a few minutes and can be the difference between a modest refund and a meaningfully larger one.
