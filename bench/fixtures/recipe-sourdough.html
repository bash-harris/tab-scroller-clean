Classic Sourdough Bread

Ingredients

You will need 500 grams of strong bread flour, 350 grams of water at room temperature, 100 grams of active sourdough starter, and 10 grams of fine sea salt. The hydration works out to roughly seventy percent, which is forgiving enough for a first attempt but still produces an open crumb.

Method

Mix the flour and water and let the dough rest for one hour before adding the starter and salt. This autolyse step lets the flour hydrate fully and makes the dough easier to handle. After the rest, incorporate the starter by pinching it through the dough until no streaks remain.

Perform four sets of stretch and folds at thirty minute intervals during the bulk fermentation, which will take between four and six hours depending on the temperature of your kitchen. The dough is ready when it has risen by about half and feels airy.

Shape the loaf, place it in a floured banneton, and refrigerate overnight. Bake at 250 degrees Celsius in a preheated Dutch oven for twenty minutes with the lid on, then a further twenty five minutes with the lid removed until the crust is deeply coloured.
