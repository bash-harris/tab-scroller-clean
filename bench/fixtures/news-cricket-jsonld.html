India beat Australia by 6 wickets in Chennai ODI

Kohli anchors the chase

India chased down a target of 287 with nine balls to spare at the M. A. Chidambaram Stadium on Wednesday, taking a 2-1 lead in the five-match one-day international series. Virat Kohli made 104 not out from 98 deliveries, his forty-ninth century in the format.

Australia had earlier posted 286 for 7 after being sent in to bat. Travis Head top-scored with 82 from 71 balls, putting on 114 for the second wicket with Steven Smith before the innings lost momentum in the middle overs against the spin of Kuldeep Yadav, who finished with figures of 3 for 48.

Bowling changes turned the game

The turning point came in the thirty-fourth over when Australia lost three wickets for eleven runs. Rohit Sharma rotated his spinners from both ends and the scoring rate fell below five an over for the remainder of the innings.

India's reply was built on an opening stand of 62 before Kohli arrived at the crease. He was watchful early, reaching his half-century from 54 balls, then accelerated once the field spread in the closing overs. The winning runs came from a clipped boundary through midwicket.

The fourth match of the series will be played in Rajkot on Saturday.
