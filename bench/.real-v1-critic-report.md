## VERDICT RATIONALE

The suite's honesty claims hold for abstains, schema, coverage, and leakage, and the pool is a faithful real export. But the gold-honesty gate fails hard: 4 of 9 duplicate-family commands close a completely unrelated tab (a YouTube video, a LeetCode problem, a different reddit post, a different ChatGPT chat) because the generator swapped pool indexes for ids; the suite even contradicts itself between L64 and L66-69. For a benchmark whose stated purpose is punishing wrong-tab selection, these labels would reward exactly the failure mode the benchmark exists to catch.
